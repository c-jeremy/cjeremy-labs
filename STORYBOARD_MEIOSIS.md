# Pip讲减数分裂 — storyboard (290 s, 84 bpm)

Logline: Pip learns why a second child of the same parents would almost certainly not be Pip, by following one
primary spermatocyte through meiosis: halving, synapsis, crossing over, random orientation, two divisions.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版 terms: 同源染色体, 联会, 四分体, 着丝粒, 赤道板, 次级精母细胞, 自由组合).

World / colour arc: warm apricot home (family) → pale lavender board (why halve) → mint-teal tissue, one big
cell (the process, one continuous world) → cream board (recap) → night indigo (the sea of combinations) →
muted mauve (when it goes wrong) → back to the warm home, now golden.
Colour code (fixed all film): father = blue `#5B8FD6`, mother = rose `#E2708C`. Papa Pip is blue, Mama Pip rose,
our plum Pip is literally their mix. Pairs: long / medium / short (the short one plays chr 21 in F).
Motif: the crowd of possible babies (A) → the sea of gametes (E) → Pip alone in the light (G).
Pip's arc: calm → confused (a sibling who isn't Pip) → wow/idea through the mechanism → dizzy at the numbers →
serious at the error → love/proud at the end.
Pacing: 1.2–1.6 s between lines, ~3 s at every section change, recap lines (L6, L17) with a held diagram after.
