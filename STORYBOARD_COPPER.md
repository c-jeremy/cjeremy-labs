# Pip讲身体里的铜 — storyboard (802 s, 90 bpm)

Logline: Pip, a genie who lives in a little copper lamp and inside our bodies, shows what a pinch of copper (four or five
grains of rice) does. It lets every cell use oxygen: at the end of the respiratory chain, three copper atoms and two
hemes hand O₂ its four electrons at once. Copper also has four side jobs. Loose copper is a poison, so the body passes
it hand to hand. The film shows what goes wrong when one of the two copper pumps breaks, and how immune cells and
medicine turn copper into a weapon.
Audience: high-school biology and chemistry, and curious college students and adults (人教版 terms: 微量元素 and the
口诀 铁猛碰新木桶, 无机盐, 有氧呼吸第三阶段, 线粒体内膜, [H], ATP合成酶, 自由基学说, 蓝细菌, 伴X隐性/常染色体隐性遗传,
铜绿, 硫酸铜溶液, 半胱氨酸, 甲硫氨酸). One step up: 细胞色素c氧化酶 (CuA, CuB, hemes), 铜锌SOD, 铜蓝蛋白,
赖氨酰氧化酶, 铜伴侣蛋白, CTR1, ATP7A/ATP7B, 金属硫蛋白, 铜死亡.

World / colour arc (the colours follow copper itself, from metal to verdigris to Cu²⁺ blue and back):
warm apricot table with the copper lamp (A1) → peach cytoplasm, one big cell with a face (A2) → cream board (A3) →
verdigris teal (B1) → rust-brown Archean sea under an orange haze that clears to blue (B2) → ocean blue (B3) →
coral mitochondrion: IMS above, matrix below (C1–C5), drained grey under cyanide (C6) → a quick montage of mini-worlds:
peach cytoplasm (D1), plasma rose (D2), fibre cream and then a farm (D3), melanocyte tan and a neuron (D4) → cream recap
board (R1) → acid magenta dark (E1) → cool lavender cytoplasm, a gut cell and a liver cell (E2, E3) → slate-mauve
(F1, F2), with a golden pasture flashback (F1) → cream recap (R2) → plasma rose, a battlefield (G1) → night navy (G2) →
the opening table again, now golden (G3, H).
Colour code (fixed all film):
- Cu⁺ = burnished copper orange `#D27A3E` bead, one + disc. Cu²⁺ = copper-sulfate blue `#3E8ED0` bead, two + discs.
  The bead changes colour when it trades an electron.
- e⁻ = small cream-yellow spark `#FFE7A0` with a − disc (glows on dark grounds).
- Iron / heme = rust red `#B8452F` (heme is a rounded square with four nubs and an Fe bead in the middle).
  Fe²⁺ / Fe³⁺ = rust bead with two / three + discs.
- O₂ = two joined sky-blue beads `#8CC8EE`. H₂O = blue droplet `#6FB2E3`. H₂O₂ = lilac dumbbell `#B79AD6`.
- Radicals (superoxide, hydroxyl) = spiky magenta star `#D2427E` with one dot (the unpaired electron). Hydroxyl is
  bigger and darker.
- H⁺ = cream disc with rose + (as in thermo). ATP = gold bead triplets (as in thermo).
- Copper chaperones = lavender clasps `#A88BD0` with yellow sulfur fingertips `#E8C547` (Cys/Met).
- Zinc = silver bead `#A9B2BC`. Cyanide = dark slate bead + violet bead joined by three ink lines.
- Proteins are pastel blobs with posture and no faces. Cells (and bacteria) are characters with faces.
Motifs:
- **The balance:** a copper pinch against four rice grains (A1) → a mountain of ATP against a body (C4) → the pinch
  again, settling (H).
- **The bead passed hand to hand:** Pip catches it (A3) → the chaperone relay (E2) → Pip hands it on, and it lands
  beside O₂ (H).
- **The 木桶:** the mnemonic (A3) → the bucket brigade (E2) → the empty and the overflowing bucket (F1, F2, R2).
- **The copper lamp:** Pip's home. Pip comes out of it (A1) and goes back in (H).
Pip, this film: a genie, not a lecturer. Pip appears only when it has a job in the mechanism: it throws Cu²⁺ into a
protein, pries a lid open, runs the relay, pulls on cyanide, conducts the iron bus. It moves Tom-and-Jerry style: big
anticipation, smears, squash and stretch, singed and blackened takes, flattened by doors. When Pip floats, a plum smoke
tail trails under it (drawn in the scene, not in pip.js). Pip stays off screen where it would only watch (D4, R1, R2,
much of F) and is gentle around disease: no slapstick near a sick child.
Pip's arc: smug genie → guilty → singed → dizzy → mischievous → curious (ancient sea) → inked → excited runner →
chased → soaked → dizzy (revolving door) → proud → straining, flung (cyanide) → janitor → sad (ALS) → conductor →
shrug → trampoline → popped balloon → blackened (explosion) → detective → juggler → traffic cop → worried, gentle (F)
→ relieved (magnet) → cheering (macrophage) → stuck in gum → smug dart → stuffed (food) → flattened (zinc door) → deep
breath → wink, back into the lamp.
Pacing: 1.2–1.6 s between lines; ~2.8 s at section changes (before L6, L12, L25, L44); 2.0–2.4 s holds for gags after
L2, L3, L18, L19, L36, L47, L58; 3.4 s after each recap (L34, L51) with the diagram held; 6.5 s after L60 for the lamp
and the iris.
