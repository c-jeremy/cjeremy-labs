# Pip大战生物圈 — storyboard (150 s, 120 bpm)

Logline: Pip, a plum sprout at the very bottom of the food chain, fights everything that tries to eat it, from a
grasshopper down to bacteria and viruses, and wins every round with real plant biology, until it yanks one link out of
the food web and the whole meadow collapses on it. Washed away to the deep sea, where its sunlight powers are useless,
Pip sees the whole biosphere as one web and stops fighting. Then the shadow from the opening comes back: it's us.
Format: a wordless short in the spirit of "Animator vs. Math". No narration, no text; score + effects. 16:9.
Audience: everyone; every gag is true biology a high-schooler can name (化学防御, 种间关系, 噬菌体, 过敏性坏死, 菌根,
  营养级联/食物网, 化能合成, 生物圈).
World / colour arc: warm spring meadow (A–C) → amber soil and pale micro-world (D–F, underground) → the meadow at noon,
  then grey-brown stubble under the swarm (G–H) → blue water sinking to indigo dark, lit only by the vent's orange (I)
  → night space and the blue Earth (J) → the meadow green again (K), dust-brown under the blade → spring light and a
  plum blossom (L).
Colour code (fixed all film): Pip plum + leaf green. Foes in acid tones: grasshopper olive, pathogenic bacteria
  violet-green, virus magenta. Friends in warm tones: ladybug red, phage steel blue, fungus cream-white, sugar gold,
  phosphate orange. Cyanide = sickly green-violet puff. The web = gold threads. Humans = shadow, ochre steel, skin.
Motif: the SHADOW. In the cold open a huge shadow rumbles over the new sprout and leaves, unexplained. It returns in K as
  the bulldozer and the hand, and in L the same hand's shadow shades Pip, now protective. Second motif: the bite notch
  in Pip's leaf (B), which is where the virus gets in (E) and what the swarm finishes (H).
Pip's arc: wonder → smug winner (B–F) → hubris (G) → ruin (H) → helpless (I) → understanding (J) → peace (K) → fear
  (K) → bloom (L).
Pacing: action on the beat (BEAT 0.5 s), holds of 1–2 s after every payoff; one rest bar between rounds.

## A  0.0–10.0  The sprout (cold open)   [in: iris from black on the seed]
  0.0   soil cross-section, a seed in the dark; iris opens on it.
  0.8   the seed coat splits; a root wiggles down, a shoot loops up.  Camera rises with the shoot.
  3.0   Pip bursts up through the soil line, crumbs flying, lands. (wow)
  5.0   first sunlight: warm glow; Pip blinks, stretches, happy → sing.
  6.6   EVENT: the light dims. A huge shadow slides over from the right, the ground shakes, low rumble. Pip looks up:
        wow → worried, sweat. We never see what casts it.
  8.6   the shadow passes, the light returns; Pip sags with relief (calm).
  [out: smash cut on the THUMP of the next shot]

## Round 1 — 个体
### B  10.0–22.0  The grasshopper (化学防御)   [in: cut on action]
  10.0  a grasshopper drops in at the right and lands, dust; Pip take (wow).
  10.8  it eyes Pip's leaf, antennae twitching, head tilting; Pip leans back (worried).
  12.6  CHOMP: a scalloped notch out of Pip's leaf. Pip angry.
  15.0  a second bite: a sickly green-violet puff bursts from the wound (plum leaves release cyanide when chewed).
  15.8  the grasshopper recoils, spits, staggers, a dizzy swirl over its head, then hops away off left.
  19.5  Pip smug, dusts its hands. Then a tickle: Pip looks at its own leaf stalk.
  [out: camera push in to the leaf, continuous into C]

### C  22.0–34.0  Aphids, ants, a ladybug (种间关系: 捕食, 互利共生)   [in: continuous push]
  22.0  close on the leaf: aphids walk on, stab their stylets in, swell. Pip strain, leaf droops.
  25.0  Pip whistles (note); a ladybug flies in, lands and eats one aphid. Pip happy.
  27.5  a line of ants marches up the stalk, taps the aphids for honeydew (a droplet), then charges the ladybug and
        nips it. The ladybug flees. (Ants farm aphids.)
  31.0  Pip, beaten, stamps its foot; then winces and looks DOWN (something at its roots).
  [out: whip down through the ground]

## Round 2 — 微观
### D  34.0–50.0  Bacteria vs. phage (噬菌体)   [in: whip down, then a scale push into a root with a cream flash]
  34.0  soil cross-section under Pip: roots from its feet. Camera dives to a root tip; cream flash at 36.0.
  36.0  micro world: the root wall, soil grains like boulders, water film. Pip (host size) beside it.
  38.0  three rod-shaped pathogenic bacteria (cells, so they have faces) whip in on flagella and latch on; the root
        darkens where they bite. Pip angry.
  41.0  Pip whistles; phages descend like landers (head, tail, tail fibres).
  42.5  one lands on a bacterium: fibres grip, the sheath contracts, a DNA squiggle runs down into it. Its face: wow →
        sick.
  45.0  it swells; inside, new phage heads assemble; it bursts (lysis) and new phages hit the other two. Pip laugh;
        the root heals.
  [out: brush wipe in meadow greens]

### E  50.0–60.0  The virus and the sacrifice (过敏性坏死)   [in: brush wipe]
  50.0  close on Pip's leaf. Magenta virus rods drift in on the wind and slip in at the BITE NOTCH.
  51.5  inset of the leaf tissue: cells; a mottled yellow patch spreads cell to cell. Pip worried, sweat.
  54.0  Pip strains: the cells around the patch turn brown and die in a ring, walling the virus in.
  56.0  the spread stops at the ring; a dry brown spot is left on the leaf. Pip winces, then wipes its brow.
  58.5  Pip slumps, exhausted (sweat).
  [out: camera sinks below the soil line with Pip's slump]

### F  60.0–68.0  The fungus friend (菌根)   [in: camera move carries through]
  60.0  soil cross-section, Pip above with roots. White hyphae thread toward the roots, branching.
  62.0  they wrap a root; orange phosphate granules run up into it; gold sugar drops run down into the fungus.
  64.5  a small mushroom pops up beside Pip. Pip powered up: a glow, leaf perks, excited.
  [out: camera rises and pulls back to the meadow]

## Round 3 — 生态系统
### G  68.0–80.0  The mistake (食物网)   [in: rise and pull back]
  68.0  meadow at noon: Pip, grass, a pond edge, a frog; flies buzz round Pip.
  69.0  the frog hops beside Pip; its tongue snaps a fly right off Pip's face. Pip startled.
  71.0  again, a slap on Pip's cheek. Pip angry.
  73.0  Pip bends a grass blade down under the frog and lets go: the frog flies off into the sky (a twinkle).
  75.0  Pip smug, dusting its hands.
  77.0  a buzz grows. One grasshopper, then another, then many (the B grasshopper among them, bent antenna). Pip's
        smile drops (worried).
  [out: cut on action, the swarm pours in]

### H  80.0–92.0  The swarm (营养级联)   [in: cut on action]
  80.0  no frogs, so the grasshoppers boom: the sky darkens with the swarm.
  83.0  they strip the meadow: grass to stubble; Pip covered, its leaf chewed to tatters.
  87.0  the swarm moves off right; the meadow is bare brown. Pip, chewed and drooping, cries.
  89.0  rain; the bare soil runs to mud and the stream sweeps Pip away.
  [out: the camera follows the water down: match into I]

## Round 4 — 生物圈
### I  92.0–110.0  The deep sea (化能合成)   [in: follow the water down]
  92.0  Pip tumbles down through blue water; light fades to indigo; marine snow.
  95.0  dark, then a glow: a black-smoker vent, a dark plume, orange heat at its base. Pip lands on rock.
  98.0  Pip tries a sunlight power-up (arms up, leaf spread, strain): nothing. No sun. Sad, "?".
  101.0 tube worms sway at the vent, red plumes, white bacterial mats: thriving without sunlight. Pip confused → wow.
  104.0 a tardigrade drifts in and bumps Pip; Pip punches it; it bounces like a gummy and paddles back, unbothered.
  108.0 Pip sits down, defeated.
  [out: the camera pulls up and out]

### J  110.0–122.0  The web (生物圈)   [in: continuous pull out]
  110.0 the camera pulls back up through the ocean to the whole Earth in the night. Pip floats beside it.
  114.0 gold threads light up around the globe linking everyone Pip fought: grasshopper, aphid, ant, ladybug,
        bacterium, phage, fungus, frog, tube worm, tardigrade, and Pip.
  116.5 callback: the frog's thread is tugged and the whole web shakes. Pip wow → idea.
  119.0 Pip opens its arms: calm. It stops fighting. The web draws it home.
  [out: zoom through the globe back down to the meadow]

## Round 5 — 人类
### K  122.0–136.0  The shadow returns   [in: zoom through]
  122.0 the meadow green again: the frog by the pond, a few grasshoppers, the ladybug, bees at a flowering tree.
        Pip waves to them (happy).
  125.0 the rumble and the shadow from the opening. A bulldozer blade enters from the right, pushing a wall of earth.
  127.0 everyone hides behind Pip; the blade scrapes the right side away: flowers and the tree go down, the bees
        flee. Pip arms out, protective. The blade stops just short. Dust.
  131.0 still. A huge human hand reaches down to the one fruit tree left at the far side: no bees, so the flowers
        withered, so there is no fruit. The fingers close on nothing.
  134.0 the hand lowers over Pip. Everyone trembles.

### L  136.0–150.0  The rhyme   [in: continuous]
  136.0 the hand scoops soil back around Pip, sets it upright, pats the soil.
  139.0 the hand stays, and its shadow shades Pip, the opening's shadow, now gentle. Pip looks up: wow → happy.
  142.0 a plum blossom opens on Pip's stalk. A bee comes back and lands on it (sparkle).
  145.0 Pip waves; the neighbours come out.
  147.5 iris out on Pip and the blossom.
