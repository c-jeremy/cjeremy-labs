cap + poly(A), 2P prolines, N1-methylpseudouridine, the LNP, endosomal escape, translation → ER → Golgi → surface,
degradation, then the lymph node (MHC II → Th → B → plasma cells/antibodies; MHC I → CTL; memory), and the real virus.

laid out by vo_mrna.mjs. Shot times follow the measured lines and their internal pauses.

extracellular blue (D) → muscle pink, then the DC's peach (E) → peach cell with violet nucleus (F) → lymph-node
green-gold (G) → back to the peach cell of A, warmer (H).

googly-eyed character) appears in A, B, C, F, H.
Text: none, except painted 5′/3′ marks, + and − charge marks, and ✓/✗.
