# Pip讲枪虾 — outline (~185 s, 92 bpm)

Agreed with the person on 2026-10-19 (one-shot mode; a 12 fps, very low-res demo on the phone before the final render;
voice Sadaltager on Gemini 3.1 with zinc's style; the person designs the cover). The zinc film is the standard for
structure, pace and gags, compressed to about three minutes.

Topic: how a pistol shrimp (枪虾 / 鼓虾, Alpheidae) hunts: the snapping claw fires a bubble, not a blow.
Audience: high-school biology and physics (压强, 沸点与气压, 肌肉), one step up: latch-and-spring power amplification,
cavitation (Versluis et al., Science 2000), shrimpoluminescence and ≥ 5000 K (Lohse et al., Nature 2001), WWII sonar
noise (Johnson et al. 1947).
Metaphor made literal: the claw is a pistol. Cocking = the hammer, the plunger = the firing pin, the bubble = the bullet,
the shockwave = the hit.

Hot open (no voice, ~10 s): a reef. Pip, the sound recordist in big headphones, holds a hydrophone up to a tiny shrimp.
SNAP: a flash, Pip blasted, sooty, the headphones smoking; deadpan; record scratch, freeze.

1. The hook (L1–L4): the shrimp, its oversized claw; the reef's crackle, loud enough to trouble WWII sonar; the
   slow-motion replay shows the claw never touched Pip. Central question: 那这一枪的子弹，到底是什么？
2. The gun (L5–L7): plunger and socket; cocking and the latch (muscles load the spring); release in about a millisecond,
   the plunger squirts a jet.
3. The bubble (L8–L11): ~25 m/s jet; fast flow, low pressure; low pressure lowers the boiling point (高原煮饭), so the
   sea water boils at room temperature: a cavitation bubble, which collapses. The 2000 high-speed film: the bang is the
   collapse, not the claw.
4. Heat and light (L12–L13): ≥ 5000 K inside the collapsing bubble; a flash too dim and brief for the eye.
5. The hunt (L14–L15): the shockwave stuns small fish and shrimp; the shrimp sees poorly, feels with its antennae,
   snaps, drags the prey into its burrow.
Recap (L16, no Pip): the chain on a board, then a hold.
Ending (L17): it wasn't the claw, it was a bubble. Pip comes back in earmuffs and goggles behind a sandbag and records
the snap cleanly; one last tiny bubble pops on its nose; wink; iris out.
