# Pip讲身体里的锌 — storyboard (854 s, 92 bpm)

Logline: Pip tries to photograph an egg at the moment of fertilisation and is blasted by the egg's own zinc fireworks.
From there: why a metal that never trades electrons sits in one protein in ten. Zinc grips hard and pulls hard on
water without ever making radicals, so it can catalyse (carbonic anhydrase, the "ping-pong" enzyme), hold proteins up
(SOD1, zinc fingers, insulin) and signal (synapses, immune cells, the egg). The same grip makes loose zinc dangerous,
so cells sponge it, gate it and sense it. Then food, phytate and yeast; too little (Prasad, acrodermatitis
enteropathica, taste) and too much (copper trapped, denture cream, Wilson's, metal fume fever). The answer comes back to
the opening: two or three grams, a ping-pong ball's weight, from the first firework on.
Audience: high-school biology and chemistry (人教版: 受精作用, 减数第二次分裂, 透明带, 有氧呼吸, 酶, 氨基酸,
半胱氨酸/组氨酸, 氢离子/氢氧根, 中性, 转录, 胰岛B细胞, 突触小泡, 负反馈), one step up: d¹⁰ and no redox, Lewis acid,
coordination numbers 4–6, CA II's ping-pong mechanism and 10⁶ s⁻¹, 3d enzyme classes, zinc fingers (Klug 1985,
TFIIIA, C2H2, 3 bp per finger), nuclear receptors, ZFNs, insulin hexamers and ZnT8, free zinc ~10⁻⁶ of total, synaptic
zinc, metallothionein (20 Cys, 7 Zn, redox release), 14 ZIP / 10 ZnT, MTF-1, phytate, ZIP4, Prasad 1958/61, AE.
This film is an experiment: **faster and denser than selenium** (81 lines, 0.9 s gaps), but the hot open gets ~10 s
with no voice so its gag reads.

Colour code (fixed all film):
- Zn²⁺ = silver bead `#A9B2BC` (copper film's) with a white crescent and two + discs. It **never changes colour**: that
  is the point. Its full shell, when shown, is a tight ring of cream electron sparks.
- Electrons = cream sparks with a − (copper). Cu⁺ copper-orange one +, Cu²⁺ blue two + (copper). Fe rust (copper),
  Se ruby (selenium), Mg pale green, Mn rose-violet.
- Water = blue droplet with two cream H dots; OH⁻ = blue bead, one H dot, a − disc; H⁺ = cream disc with a rose +.
- CO₂ = charcoal C bead between two sky-blue O beads; HCO₃⁻ = a charcoal C with three blue O and one H, small triangle.
- Cys = yellow S bead; His = teal ring bead. Proteins are pastel blobs with posture and no faces (pockets, arms).
- Radicals = magenta spiky star with a dot (copper). H₂O₂ = lilac dumbbell (copper).
- Free zinc = a glowing silver spark; bound zinc = a bead in a pocket.
- Three verdict icons (from C1 to the end): a shield (no radicals), a clamp (the grip), a tug rope (the pull).
