# Clawd讲PCR (149.5 s)

Logline: Clawd has one tiny piece of DNA and wants a million, so it climbs into a PCR tube and shows how the copier
works: denature, anneal, extend; then the two exam traps: the target-length product only appears in cycle 3
(2ⁿ − 2n after n cycles), primers with a 5′ tail (restriction site) still need 3 cycles for a full double-stranded
product, tails go on the 5′ end only, and a run of n cycles uses 2ⁿ⁺¹ − 2 primers.

Voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural (edge-tts, +4%), 14 lines (assets/vo_pcr/lNN.wav), the same voice as the DNA video.
Shot times follow the measured lines and their internal pauses.

World: a warm lab bench → inside the tube, where **the water's colour is the temperature**: room teal → hot coral
(denature) → cold blue (anneal) → golden (extend). A painted thermometer sits at the screen edge and three
cycle pips fill on it. The end is back at the bench, the tube glowing gold with copies.
Motif: strands are tapered ribbons, the point is always the 3′ end. Template = blue, new strand = gold, forward
primer = green, reverse primer = violet, restriction tail = rose (with a tiny painted scissors when introduced).
Two chalk guide lines mark the target's two ends; a strand that fits exactly between them is a target.
Text: none except painted brush-stroke marks the subject needs: 5′ / 3′ at strand ends, and the two formulas
(2ⁿ−2n, 2ⁿ⁺¹−2). Big ✓ and ✗ are painted marks.

Clawd's arc: proud → hopeful → excited → hot/sweaty → determined (no helicase) → cold/shivering → happy riding Taq →
proud → surprised (trap!) → thinking → starstruck (two targets) → determined (second trap) → confused (tail flaps)
→ proud → thinking (counting) → love/excited at the end.
