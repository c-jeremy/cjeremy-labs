# Pip讲玉米雇杀手 — storyboard (295 s, 88 bpm)

Logline: Pip watches a caterpillar get killed by a wasp out of nowhere and wants to know who sent it. The trail leads
back to the maize: the caterpillar's own spit tips the plant off, the plant sends a chemical call, and the wasp answers.
Then Pip pulls back to the whole ecosystem: neighbours listening in, balance and negative feedback, a call under the
ground, and farmers using the same trick.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版 选择性必修2: 生产者 / 初级消费者, 食物链, 种间关系·寄生, 化学信息, 信息源 / 信道 /
信息受体, 负反馈调节, 生物防治).

World / colour arc: hazy morning field, gold-green (the crime) → pale pea-green inside the leaf (the spit) → bright
midday sky with amber plumes over the field (the call) → warm macro leaf, with a rosy cutaway inside the caterpillar
(the kill) → cream board (recap) → late-afternoon teal-gold field from above (the ecosystem) → umber soil cross-section
(underground and farming) → sunset over the opening field, everything gold (the ending).
Colour code (fixed all film):
- maize = sap green `#7FAE5A`, dark `#4E7A3A`
- caterpillar = olive `#9BAA4A` with a cream side stripe
- **the caterpillar's tell** (spit, and the elicitor molecule's caterpillar half) = magenta-violet `#B0559A`
- **the call** (maize volatiles, above and below ground) = amber `#E7A93C`: soft wisps, never a glow on the light
  sky, a `glow()` only at dusk and underground
- wasp = ink-dark body, amber-orange legs; nematodes = pale cream; rootworm larva = ivory with a brown head
Motif: **the amber wisp.** In shot A it already curls up from the maize, faint and unremarked. That's the answer, hidden
in plain sight. In C we learn what it is; in G the whole sunset field is full of wisps and Pip can finally "hear" them.
Pip's arc: worried (a fellow plant being eaten) → startled (the wasp) → question (who sent it?) → wow (the spit
experiment) → laugh (the irony) → idea (化学信息) → excited (riding along with the wasp) → shy/queasy (parasitism) →
smug (a good deal for the maize) → calm (recap) → love (neighbours warned) → determined (feedback) → sad (the lost
ability) → happy (it's back) → sniff, smile, wave (the ending).
Pacing: 1.2 s between lines, 2.6–3.4 s at every section change, recap L13 followed by a 3.4 s hold on its diagram.

## Character design (concrete, painted, not fussy)

- **Maize plant:** a jointed stalk (a tapered `ribbon` with darker node rings every ~110 px), long strap leaves that
  arch and droop, each one `ribbon` with a lighter midrib line, wrapping the stalk at the base with a sheath; a tassel of
  thin dry-brush spikes on top; a mid-stalk ear wrapped in husk with a tuft of rose-tan silk. No face: maize acts only
  through sway, posture and its wisps. Close-up leaf: a broad tapered blade with parallel veins (3–5 thin ink lines)
  and a chewed, ragged edge.
- **Caterpillar** (beet armyworm larva): a segmented body (one tapered `ribbon` along a wavy path, with 10–12 faint
  segment creases), a cream side stripe, a rounder darker head capsule with tiny mandibles, three pairs of small true
  legs at the front and stubby prolegs behind. It crawls in a looping wave (`seg` per segment) and munches with a head
  bob. No face.
- **Wasp** (*Cotesia*): head + two long curved antennae that keep twitching, thorax, a pinched waist, an oval
  abdomen, two pairs of translucent veined wings (pale wash, fine ink veins) that blur when flying, orange legs. On
  the leaf it taps with its antennae, then curls its abdomen under to sting.
- **Rootworm larva:** ivory C-shaped grub with a brown head. **Nematodes:** pale cream thread-worms that wriggle in
  sine waves.
- **Molecule (L5–6):** a long zig-zag chain (the fatty acid, maize green) joined to a small bead cluster (the amino
  acid, magenta). The "receptor" is a cupped notch on a leaf cell's membrane.
- **Pip** stands on soil or sits on a leaf beside the action (`u` 30–34, 60+ in close-ups), never over it.
