# Pip讲体温调节 — storyboard (290 s, 96 bpm)

Logline: Pip gets fooled by a chili and a mint leaf, and learns how the body really senses cold and heat (TRPV1 and
TRPM8 channels), where the thermostat is (the hypothalamus) and what it does about it: less heat lost, more heat made
(shivering, hormones, brown fat's UCP1 "back door"), sweat that carries heat away, all as one negative-feedback loop.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版 terms: 冷觉/热觉感受器, 下丘脑 体温调节中枢, 大脑皮层, 甲状腺激素, 肾上腺素, 负反馈),
plus one step up: TRP channels, Na⁺/Ca²⁺ influx, UCP1, ATP synthase.
Tone: Tom-and-Jerry. Every fact lands on Pip's body as an exaggerated transformation (burns red and breathes fire,
freezes into an ice block, grows porcupine hair, vibrates to a beat, glows like a stove, melts into a puddle). Three
quizzes break the fourth wall: Pip turns to the viewer, a painted pie timer drains for 3 s, ding.

World / colour arc: warm apricot kitchen (A) → Pip's own plum skin under a magnifier, then the nerve membrane, split warm
(left, TRPV1) / cool (right, TRPM8) (B) → lavender brain (C) → cold night-blue snow, then the amber inside of a brown fat
cell (D) → blazing ochre desert that fogs up into a grey-teal steam bath (E) → cream recap board (F) → the kitchen again,
now golden (G).
Colour code (fixed all film): hot = coral `#E4604E`, cold = ice blue `#6FB4E0`. TRPV1 coral, TRPM8 ice blue. Na⁺ = ochre
disc, Ca²⁺ = violet disc, H⁺ (protons) = cream disc with a rose +. Capsaicin red-orange, menthol mint. ATP = gold beads.
Heat = orange wavy lines and glow. Blood = red tube. Hot Pip goes coral, cold Pip goes ice blue.
Motif: the chili (A: fooled; B: the Nobel gag; G: fooled again on purpose). The quiz timer (B, C, E).
Pip's arc: happy → fooled (fire / ice) → confused → wow/idea at the channels → smug → shivering, embarrassed (bumps),
dancing (抖音), laughing warm (燃脂) → worried, melting (sauna) → idea at the recap → smug, then fire again, wink.
Pacing: ~1 s between lines, ~2 s at section changes, 3.5 s countdowns after L3, L10, L19; gags get their own holds
after L8, L12, L17, L20 and L22.
