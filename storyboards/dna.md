# Clawd讲转录和翻译 (88 s)

Logline: Clawd shrinks through a microscope into a cell to show how a gene becomes a protein: DNA is unzipped and
copied into messenger RNA (transcription), the RNA is capped, tailed, spliced and sent out of the nucleus, and a
ribosome reads it three bases at a time while tRNAs bring amino acids (translation), until a stop codon releases a
chain that folds into a working protein.

Voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural (edge-tts), 10 lines; the shot timings follow their measured lengths and pauses.
World: one cell, one journey inward then outward. Lab bench (warm wood, daylight) → microscope field (pale teal) →
nucleus (deep violet, cool) → through a nuclear pore → cytoplasm (warm peach, golden) → a clean paper summary board.
Colour arc: cool inside the nucleus, warming as the message travels out and becomes a protein.

Teaching language (no captions): bases are coloured puzzle pieces whose ends interlock, purines pointed/round tabs,
pyrimidines matching notches. A orange ▲ fits T teal ▼; G green ● fits C violet ◡; U is rose with T's notch.
Their letters are PAINTED on the pieces (brush strokes that boil), only at the moments the narration names them
(A-T, C-G, the T→U swap, A-U-G). DNA backbone blue, mRNA backbone gold. Characters: RNA polymerase (a teal blob that
hugs the DNA), ribosome (two plum subunits that sandwich the mRNA), tRNAs (yellow cloverleaves carrying a bead),
the finished protein (a folded bead-chain with a face). Clawd is the guide riding along.
