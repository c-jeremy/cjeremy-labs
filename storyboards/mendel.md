# Pip讲孟德尔遗传定律 — storyboard (509 s, 88 bpm)

Logline: Pip mixes red and white paint and gets pink, so it believes heredity is paint, until a red drop comes back
from nowhere. Following Mendel's pea seeds, Pip (and the viewer) re-derive both laws by hand: the hidden wrinkled
seed returns 3:1, so the factors must be marbles, in pairs, split one per gamete; a testcross checks it; then two
traits split independently, 3:1 × 3:1 = 9:3:3:1, and the marbles turn out to ride on chromosomes.
Audience: someone who has never met Mendel, up to high-school biology (人教版 terms: 性状, 相对性状, 纯种, 杂交, 自交,
去雄, 套袋, 显性/隐性性状, 性状分离, 遗传因子, 配子, 测交, 假说—演绎法, 分离定律, 自由组合定律, 基因, 同源染色体).

World / colour arc: warm cream paint table (the paint idea) → sage-green monastery garden in morning light (the
experiment) → pale blue-grey "thinking board" (the derivation, one continuous world) → cream recap → amber inside a
seed (what r really is) → the garden again, late-afternoon gold (two traits) → pale blue board again (the counting,
the square) → cream (the Punnett grid) → mint cell (chromosomes, a callback to Pip讲减数分裂) → the paint table at
golden light, now with a box of marbles.

Colour code (fixed all film):
- Peas: round = smooth ball with a highlight; wrinkled = crumpled outline with crease lines. Seed colour yellow
  `#EBCB5A` / green `#9CC25E` (all yellow until two traits come in).
- Factors are glass **marbles** (never letters): shape R = deep blue `#4469B8`, r = pale blue `#B4CFEE` with a
  crinkle line; colour Y = gold `#E7B12F`, y = green `#6FA548`. Darker/strong = dominant.
- Gametes: pollen = small spiky orange grain `#F0A34A`; egg = soft pink oval `#F4B9B4`; each carries one marble.
- Paint: red `#D84A4E`, white `#FBF3E4`, pink `#EE9DAA`, the muddy average `#C9A99E`.
Motif: **paint vs marbles.** Paint merges and can't come back; marbles click together and stay themselves. The red
drop that "comes back" in L2 returns in L27 as a red marble, explained.
Ponder beats: after L5, L6, L10, L11, L13 (longest), L15, L20, L22 the picture holds on the question (a "?" on the
thing in doubt, Pip thinking, chin on hand), nothing new arrives, and the music plays its "think" phrase.
Pip's arc: happy painter → confused (the red drop) → curious gardener in Mendel's hat → thinking at every ponder →
wow/idea at each answer → excited at 3:1 → smug at the testcross → dizzy at four numbers → idea at the two 3:1s →
love at the end, holding a marble.
Pacing: 1.4–1.6 s between lines, ~3 s at section changes, 4.5–6 s ponder holds, 3.5 s after the recap.
