# Pip讲身体里的硒 — storyboard (792.7 s, 86 bpm)

Logline: Pip binges on Brazil nuts and loses its leaf, and the film rewinds to find out why such a touchy element is
worth a rewritten genetic code. Selenium sits in the 21st amino acid, which the ribosome slips in at a stop codon. Its
trick is that it can be oxidised and come straight back, where sulfur gets burnt. That lets glutathione peroxidases,
above all GPx4, keep membranes from catching fire (ferroptosis). Then the soil decides how much we get: too little
(Keshan disease, a virus that turns nasty) or too much (hooves, hair and nails falling off, garlic breath).
Audience: high-school biology and chemistry, then a step up for college students and adults (人教版 terms:
组成人体蛋白质的21种氨基酸, 密码子/终止密码子UGA, 核糖体, tRNA, 同主族, 氧化/还原, 有氧呼吸, 过氧化氢, 自由基学说,
甲状腺激素与碘, 细胞膜的磷脂双分子层, 不饱和脂肪酸). One step up: SECIS, 终止因子, Ser→Sec on tRNA, GPx and the
GSH cycle, overoxidation, GPx4 and 铁死亡 (2012), Ingold 2018, deiodinases, TrxR's C-terminal Sec, SELENOP (10 Sec)
and ApoER2, the selenoprotein hierarchy, Keshan disease and the Mianning trial, Beck's coxsackievirus, selenosis.
This film is an experiment: **more slapstick than copper and a hot open**, with every gag acting out a mechanism step.

Colour code (fixed all film):
- Se = ruby bead `#C8323C` with a cream crescent highlight. States: ready (a small cream H dot on it), oxidised (a
  sky-blue O bead stuck on top), bound to glutathione (joined to a yellow S bead), all as visible state on the drawing.
- S = sulfur yellow `#E8C547`. Burnt sulfur (overoxidised for good) = charcoal grey `#5A5560` with two or three O beads
  stuck on, smoking. Cys in a chain = yellow bead, Sec = ruby bead.
- O = sky blue `#8CC8EE`. H₂O = blue droplet. H₂O₂ = lilac dumbbell `#B79AD6` (as in copper).
- Radicals = spiky magenta star with one dot `#D2427E` (as in copper); hydroxyl bigger and darker; superoxide smaller.
- Fe = rust-brown bead `#A0522D` with + discs. Iodine = violet bead `#7B5CC4`.
- Lipid peroxidation = little flames (red, orange, yellow) on the lipid tails; a quenched lipid gets a blue O-H tag.
- GSH = three linked beads with a yellow sulfur in the middle (copper's gsh()).
- Traffic light: red = stop, green = go (painted lamps, no text).
- Proteins are pastel blobs with posture and no faces. Cells, Pip and animals (dot eyes) are the only faces.

- **The 不倒翁 (roly-poly toy):** introduced (B2, it bonks Pip) → the GPx cycle and the boxing match (D1) → recap (R1)
  → rocking all day (K).
- **Red light / green light:** the selenium-ruby signal glass (B2) → UGA (C2) → "生命改写终止密码子" (E2) → a green
  light for the virus (H2) → 停→行 (K).

- **Pip's leaf** (the running gag, tied to hair loss): falls off in the hot open (A1, undone by the rewind) → bleached
  blond by H₂O₂ (D1) → charred in the fire (E1, E2) → green again after the wipe (F) → falls off again (I3, nailed back
  on: a nail head stays in it through I5) → the nailed leaf drops and a fresh sprout regrows (K).
- **The moon:** crescent in the window (A1) → the moon goddess (B1) → full at the end (K).
Pip, this film: Tom-and-Jerry slapstick, pushed further than copper. Pip does a job in every mechanism it appears in
(courier, magician, coach, referee, firefighter, dentist, dragon dancer, farrier) and every gag is a step of the
chemistry. A cartoon sound for each gag. Pip sits out both recaps, the mice (E2), Keshan (H1, H2) and the Enshi
villagers (I2); a gag right after each gentle stretch releases it.
Pip's arc: greedy muncher → mortified (leaf gone) → wobbly → curious → nearly run over → bonked → racing → flattened
→ magician, burnt fingers → overwhelmed counter → bleached, horrified → slot-machine glee → referee, KO'd →
firefighter, singed → solemn (red→green) → dentist, crash → toasted → bleached toe → whipped → dragon dancer, gate-slammed
→ juggler → (recap) → gardener, outwitted → (Keshan, off) → relieved → farrier, hammered thumb → anxious about the
leaf, bonked → backfired, blackened → garlic-mortified → (recap) → munching → sprayed, sneezing → buried in pills →
sensible → calm, balanced → bow, wink, wave.
