# Pip讲枪虾 — storyboard (192.4 s, 92 bpm)

Logline: Pip, recording the reef's crackle with a hydrophone, holds it up to a tiny shrimp, and the bang through its
headphones throws it head over heels, though nothing touched it. The replay asks what the bullet was. Pip then works the
shrimp's gun step by step: cocks the claw against a spring (and is blasted by the jet), boils cold water without a fire,
catches the bubble in a jar (and it bangs), toasts a marshmallow at a bubble hotter than the Sun's surface (and it comes
back untouched: the heat lasts an instant), is stunned beside a passing fish. The ending: it was never the claw, it was
the bubble collapsing; Pip, ready this time, records the snap.
Science notes (from the science check): the shock stuns only prey within a few millimetres to a centimetre or two, so
Pip is never more than one or two claw lengths away when it is hit; the bubble forms in the jet just in front of the
claw and collapses within about a centimetre: it never flies at the target; the flash is too dim to see and the heat
stays inside the bubble, so nothing is burnt or sooted by it; the claw shuts in under a millisecond; the energy is
loaded into a spring and released by a latch.
Audience: high-school biology and physics (人教版: 节肢动物, 肌肉, 压强, 沸点与气压, 能量), one step up: latch-and-spring
power amplification, cavitation (空化; Versluis et al., Science 2000), shrimpoluminescence (虾致发光; ≥ 5000 K, Lohse
et al., Nature 2001), WWII sonar noise (Johnson, Everest & Young 1947).
This film follows the zinc film's shape and pace at a fifth of its length: a ~10 s wordless hot open with a record
scratch, an instant replay, one central question, every gag a step of the mechanism, one recap with a hold, and an
ending that answers the opening with its own image.
World / colour arc (the shrimp's own sea): reef turquoise in daylight (A) → a pale aqua study tank for the gun (B) →
deep night blue for the bubble, where the flash and heat can glow (C, D) → reef turquoise at dusk for the hunt (E) →
cream board (R) → the reef again, warm sunset (K).
Colour code (fixed all film):

  `#9E3E28` / `#7A2E1E`; highlights of a part are a warm glow, never a label.

- The shockwave: cream rings. The flash: cyan-white light. Sound: painted arcs only.
- Pressure: a gauge with a blue (low) → red (high) arc and a needle, no numbers.
- The muscle: a striated red band inside the cut-away palm; it darkens and bulges as it loads.
Motifs:
- **The bullet that isn't the claw:** the hot open → the replay with no contact (L4) → the bubble (L9–L11) → the
  recap → the answer (L17).
- **Pip's headphones and hydrophone** (the running gag): blasted in the open; ears rattled by the crackle (L3); worn
  again for the high-speed playback (L11); in the ending swapped for earmuffs, goggles and a sandbag, and the recording
  finally works.
- **The gun made literal:** cocking = the hammer drawn back and the latch clicking; the plunger = the firing pin; the
  jet = the muzzle; the bubble = the bullet; the shock rings = the hit.
