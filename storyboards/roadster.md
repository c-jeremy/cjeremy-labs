# Pip讲会倒挂的车 — storyboard (270 s, 92 bpm)

Logline: Pip watches a car hang head-down from the top of a curled ramp, then let go and fly. To find out how, Pip
works through why a car can stand still at all (f ≤ μN), why it can't on a steep slope or a wall (tanθ ≤ μ, N → 0),
how a loop gets away with it (N + mg = mv²/r), and where the Roadster gets its N instead (ΔP·A). The same air that
pins it down then throws it off (Newton's third law), and the film ends on wanting cool things again.
Audience: high-school physics (人教版 必修一 重力 / 弹力 / 摩擦力、受力分析、牛顿运动定律; 必修二 圆周运动、向心力).

World / colour arc: night test site, indigo with sodium-gold floodlights (the hang) → cream physics board with a faint
blue grid (friction, slopes) → pale noon sky board (the loop) → airy pale blue (the air) → warm afternoon asphalt
(the launch) → back to the night ramp (the flight) → the same ramp at dawn, rose-gold (the ending).
Colour code (fixed all film), arrows are painted ribbons with a head, never labelled:
- gravity mg = ochre `#E3A23B`; its components = the same ochre, paler and dashed
- normal force N = teal `#2F9C96` (the motif)
- friction f = rose `#D45C73`; its limit μN = a pale rose ghost arrow behind it
- air / pressure push = sky blue `#6FA8D6` (small arrows, motes)
- thrust jets = cream puffs; the Roadster = pearl white with dark glass; the ordinary car = slate blue; the stunt car =
  orange
Motif: **the teal N arrow.** It sits under every car that holds on, shrinks to nothing on the wall, grows from speed
in the loop, grows from air under the Roadster, and vanishes again at the moment it lets go.
Pip's arc: wow (the hang) → startled (it flies) → question → calm, idea (f ≤ μN) → worried (the slope) → sad (it falls
off) → excited (the loop) → worried (too slow) → calm (recap) → wow (the air) → smug (only 5%) → excited (the launch)
→ wow (the flight, understood) → shy (flying cars as a joke) → love (it's real) → happy, wave.
Pacing: 1.4 s between lines; 3.2–3.6 s at section changes; recap L10 then a 4.2 s hold; the ending gets 1.6 s
between its last two lines and ~5 s after.
