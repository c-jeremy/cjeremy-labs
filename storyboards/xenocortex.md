# Pip讲"人脑"小鼠 — storyboard (311 s, 88 bpm)

Logline: Pip meets a lab mouse whose cortex is more than 90% human, and learns the trick that made it possible (empty
the room first, then move the human cells in), and the surprises that followed: the wiring, the synchronised firing,
a neuron type no mouse has, and what the model is for.
Audience: high-school biology and curious adults (人教版 terms: 大脑皮层, 丘脑, 脑干, 脊髓, 神经元, 轴突, 免疫, 干细胞),
plus one step up: organoid, von Economo neuron, cohesion ("glue" on sister chromatids, a callback to Pip讲减数分裂).

World / colour arc: warm sand maze, seen from above (A) → lavender board, brain maps (B) → mint tissue: one cell, then
the brain map, the mouse, the guards (C) → peach lab bench, the dish (D1) → mint brain map, the transplant (D2) →
cream recap board (R) → lights out: night-navy fluorescence (E1–E3) → lavender board, the Y-maze (E4) → dim mauve,
hypoxia, then a cool grey "limits" board (F) → the sand maze again, now golden (G).
Colour code (fixed all film): human tissue = rose `#DC7FA0` (Pip's plum family); mouse tissue = slate `#93A5BB`; blood
vessels = brick red `#C4483F`; human axons = glowing rose threads, mouse axons = teal `#4FB3A6`; cohesin glue = gold.
The lab mouse is white with pink ears, nose and tail; a dot eye, no other face (STYLE: faces belong to Pip and cells).
Motif: the rose flood over the cortex. Opening (A, 92% in a heartbeat) → the squeezed patch in the rat (B) → the empty
dashed room (B, C) → the slow, earned flood (D2) → the same mouse in the same maze with a faint rose glow (G).
Pip's arc: calm → huge wow (A) → confused (B) → idea (B) → shy "shh" at the gene → sad at the dying cell → wow at
the half brain → tiptoe past the guards → excited at the dish → worried at the dead core → strain, smug, love as it
grows → idea at the recap → awe in the dark → goosebumps → curious at the maze → worried at hypoxia → serious at the
red line → wink and wave.
Pacing: 1.3 s between lines; 2.8 s at section changes (before L3, L6, L10, L20, L22); 1.8 s after the hook; 3.4 s
after the recap (L14) with its diagram held; the dark fluorescence stretch keeps the camera slow.

Brain map: a flat side-view (sagittal) mouse brain, nose right: olfactory bulb, cortex dome, hippocampus under it,
striatum, thalamus, midbrain, cerebellum, brainstem → spinal cord leaving left. The hook and the ending use the top
(dorsal) view, where the cortex is almost all you see.
