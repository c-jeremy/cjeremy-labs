# Pip讲植物吸收二氧化碳 — storyboard (320 s, 90 bpm)

Logline: Pip, a sprout itself, finds that every breath of CO₂ through its leaf costs hundreds of water molecules. It
follows one CO₂ through the stoma into the chloroplast (C3: Rubisco and the Calvin cycle), sees why Rubisco struggles
in the heat (slow, grabs O₂: photorespiration), then meets two fixes: corn's CO₂ pump split in space (C4, the Kranz
wreath) and the cactus's night shift split in time (CAM). The scale between food and water finally balances.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版 terms: 气孔, 保卫细胞, 叶肉细胞, 叶绿体基质, 光反应, 暗反应, CO₂的固定,
C3 / C5, 还原, 卡尔文循环, ATP, NADPH, 光呼吸, 维管束鞘细胞, 花环型结构, PEP羧化酶, C4植物, 液泡, 苹果酸, CAM植物),
one step above in places (Rubisco by name, photorespiration, Heyne's sour leaves, the C4-rice project).

World / colour arc: hot noon meadow (the dilemma) → leaf-green epidermis (the door) → pale green leaf interior →
lilac lab bench (Calvin's lollipop) → cool mint stroma (the cycle, one continuous world) → the stroma turning hot
apricot (the flaw) → cream recap → corn-gold field → sage Kranz cross-section (the pump) → sand-gold desert at dawn
→ the CAM cell: indigo night outside, then gold day → cream recap → pale sky board (the world's share) → the opening
meadow again, now split: corn in the sun, cactus under the moon.
Colour code (fixed all film):
- CO₂ = a charcoal carbon `#3F3A47` between two coral oxygens `#E4675A`; O₂ = two coral beads; water = sky-blue drops.
- Carbon chains (C3, C4, C5) = charcoal beads on an ink link; a carbon just fixed from CO₂ wears a gold ring
  `#F4C54A` (¹⁴C in Calvin's flask glows the same gold).
- Rubisco = olive-teal `#5E9C86` (the big slow enzyme with a cleft); PEP carboxylase = orange `#EE9A4C` (small, quick).
- ATP = ochre bead with three phosphate dots; NADPH = violet bead carrying a cream H-dot. Sugar = honey hexagon.
- Bundle-sheath cells deep green `#5E9E58`; mesophyll cells light green `#B5D88F`; vacuole pale blue, sour malate lime `#D7E36A`.
Motif: **the scale** (天平): food (one CO₂) on one pan, water on the other. L1 it tips hard to water; L15 two scales
(C4 half the pile); L19 three scales (CAM, a sliver); L22 it settles level beside Pip.
Pip's arc: hot and sweaty → confused (two choices) → wow at the door → idea (fixation) → happy with the cycle →
impatient/worried at Rubisco → wow at the corn → smug/excited with the pump → sour face at the leaf, confused →
sleepy at night, idea at dawn → proud at the recap → love, wave.
Pacing: 1.4–1.6 s between lines; 2.2 s after the hook question (L1); ~3 s into each new section (before L4, L8,
L11, L16, L20); recap lines L10 and L20 with ~3.5 s held after them.
