# Pip讲消失的三年 — storyboard (384 s, 84 bpm)

Logline: Pip finds that the first three pearls on its own string of memories are blank, and wants to know whether those
years were never written or were written and locked away. The old textbook answer (an unripe hippocampus) gives way to
the 2025 infant fMRI study, the mouse engram experiments bring "forgotten" memories back with light, and two guesses
(a brain under construction, a key that no longer fits) explain why we can't get in. A twist on false first memories,
then the locked door, and a teaser: the building work starts again in adolescence.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版 选择性必修1 "学习与记忆": 短时记忆 · 海马, 长时记忆 · 突触形态和功能的改变 ·
新突触的建立), plus 情景记忆 and 婴儿期遗忘, introduced in the film.

World / colour arc: warm apricot home (Pip's string of memories, baby Pip with Papa and Mama) → soft peach nursery board
(what babies learn) → dusky lilac brain (the hippocampus, the old answer) → cool clinic teal (Yale, 2025) → warm sand lab
(the mice, the light; the opto film's world) → cream board (recap) → ochre building site inside the hippocampus (why we
can't get in) → sepia album (false first memories) → back to the opening home, now golden at dusk (the locked door).
Colour code (fixed all film):
- an episodic memory = a **pearl**: warm cream-gold glass with a tiny painted scene inside; a **blank pearl** = grey-lilac,
  hollow, no scene
- skills and rules = sap green (they stay)
- hippocampus = coral rose `#E58A7A`, a seahorse curl; a young one is thin and pale
- the locked door and the key = indigo-violet and gold
- light (optogenetics) = the opto film's cornflower blue; engram cells = gold
- new neurons = mint; synapses = small ochre bulbs
- Papa = blue, Mama = rose (the meiosis film's parents); baby Pip is our plum Pip, small
Motif: **the string of pearls** with three blank ones at the start (A). The blank pearls come back in B (婴儿期遗忘), as
"stored" in D, behind the door in E's recap and in F, and in H the camera goes behind the door: they're glowing.
Pip's arc: calm → confused (blank pearls) → laugh (baby antics) → sad (the lost kind) → smug (the textbook answer) →
wow (ask the babies) → excited → worried (取?) → excited (the light switch) → wow (the mouse remembers) → cautious →
idea (recap) → strain (building site, shears) → worried (the key) → proud → shy (a fake memory) → love (the door) → wave.
Pacing: 1.4 s between lines, 3.0–3.4 s at section changes, 4.2 s after the recap (L18), 3.6 s before the ending.

## Props (concrete, painted, not fussy)

- **Pearl:** a glass orb (cream-gold wash, ink outline, a cream highlight crescent) with one tiny scene: sun over a hill,
  a heart, a ball, a house, a cake with candles, a flower, a canyon. A blank pearl: grey-lilac, dashed outline, empty.
- **Hippocampus:** a seahorse curl (a tapered ribbon that rolls into a tail spiral, a head with a snout), coral rose,
  inside a side-view brain (cerebrum with gyri, the striped cerebellum, the brainstem). A real seahorse swims up to it once.
- **Door:** an arched indigo door with gold studs and a keyhole, set into the hippocampus wall. The key: gold.
- **MRI scanner:** a big cream donut with a sliding bed; a screen above the bore for pictures; a brain-slice monitor.
- **Mouse:** the opto film's mouse (cutaway brain window, the fibre with blue light), plus a pup version. The chamber:
  a box with striped walls and a grid floor.
- **Building site:** neurons (soma + dendrites + axon) linked by ochre synapse bulbs; Pip in a hard hat with garden shears.
