# Pip讲三羧酸循环 — storyboard (267.5 s, 90 bpm)

Logline: Pip breathes out and wonders where the carbon in its breath came from; it follows one bite of breakfast
from glucose to pyruvate to acetyl-CoA and once round the citric-acid cycle, counts what one turn makes, learns why a
cycle that never touches oxygen still stops without it, and ends back at the table, breathing out the cycle's carbon.
Audience: high-school biology (人教版: 有氧呼吸三个阶段, 细胞质基质, 线粒体基质/内膜, 丙酮酸, 二氧化碳, ATP) and a step
above (乙酰辅酶A, 草酰乙酸, 柠檬酸, 异柠檬酸, α-酮戊二酸, 琥珀酰辅酶A, 琥珀酸, 延胡索酸, 苹果酸, NADH, FADH₂, GTP, 脱羧).

World / colour arc: warm apricot breakfast table (A) → rosy-peach cytoplasm (B) → cool mint mitochondrial matrix, one
continuous world with the cycle as a painted wheel (C) → cream board, the tally (D) → the inner membrane: pale apricot
above, mint below, a grey-blue chill when the oxygen is gone (E) → sepia 1937 paper warming to gold (F) → the breakfast
table again, now golden (G).
Colour code (fixed all film):
- carbon = a bead. Carbons from food (glucose → acetyl) are **gold**; the cycle's own carrier carbons (oxaloacetate)
  are **teal**. Oxygen = a small soft-red bead; hydrogen = a small cream bead.
- a carboxyl group = a carbon with a V of two red beads; decarboxylation = that group leaving as CO₂ (C + 2 O).
- CO₂ always floats up and out of frame (toward the breath).
- NAD⁺ = a pale-blue two-lobed shuttle with an empty notch → NADH = deep blue with a glowing pair of electrons.
  FAD = pale flavin yellow → FADH₂ = amber, loaded. GTP: green base + three coral phosphates; ATP: ochre base.
- coenzyme A = a mint ribbon with a yellow sulfur hook. Each intermediate has its own soft backing colour on the wheel.
Motif: the breath with CO₂ in it (A, G) and the turning wheel; the gold carbons that stay on the wheel (C → D).
Pip's arc: happy (breakfast) → wow (carbon in its breath) → confused → idea (the wheel) → curious/wow through the
mechanism → dizzy after one lap → smug at the tally, confused at "only one ATP" → wink at the secret → excited at the
membrane → worried when oxygen is gone, relieved → sad at the rejection, laughing at the Nobel → love → wave → iris out.
Pacing: 1.1–1.3 s between lines, 2.4–2.8 s at section changes, a held lap after L11 and after L14.
