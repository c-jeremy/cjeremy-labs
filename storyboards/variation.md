# Pip讲变异与伴性遗传 — storyboard (369 s, 88 bpm)

Logline: Pip follows the "royal disease" from Queen Victoria's nursery to the Romanov bones to learn two things: where
a disease nobody had before comes from (one base-pair substitution in one germ cell: 基因突变), and why it almost only
strikes boys (the F9 gene sits on the X, and a boy has no backup X: 伴X隐性遗传).
Audience: middle to high school biology (人教版 terms: 基因突变, 碱基对的替换/增添/缺失, 等位基因, 体细胞/生殖细胞,
基因重组, 染色体变异, 性染色体, 携带者, 伴性遗传, 交叉遗传, 伴X隐性/显性).

World / colour arc: royal crimson-and-gold palace (the mystery) → rose blood vessel (what fails) → deep indigo nucleus
(the one base pair) → pale mint workshop board (what a mutation is, where it comes from) → cream recap → soft lilac
board (X and Y, the crosses, the pedigree) → cream recap → the palace again, now at dusk-gold (the answer) → warm liver
peach (the fix) → iris out on Pip.
Colour code (fixed all film):
- X chromosome teal `#4FA39B`; Y chromosome slate violet `#8C86C2` (small).
- The F9 locus on the X: a **gold** band when it works, a **brick-red** band when it carries the mutation.
- Factor IX (the protein) = gold beads, the same gold as its gene; a broken one = a short red-brown stub.
- Bases (puzzle-stub pieces from the DNA film): A ochre, T teal, G sap green, C violet; the mutated pair glows.
- Royals are Pips with little gold crowns. Victoria lavender, Albert blue; an affected boy shows a purple bruise.
Motif: **the family tree with a red thread.** L1 a red mark appears from nowhere and runs down into boys only; L3 the
tree fills out across four houses; L18 the same thread zigzags male → female → male; L22–24 it returns, now explained,
and the British branch stays clean.
Pip's arc: curious → confused (the question) → worried (the bleeding) → wow/idea (one base pair) → thinking through the
mechanism → surprised (Victoria is fine?) → idea (no backup) → worried at the numbers → proud at the answer → happy, wave.
Pacing: 1.4 s between lines; ~3 s into each new section (after L3, L6, L23); 2.2 s after the hook question (L1);
recap lines L13 and L21 with ~3.4 s held after them.
